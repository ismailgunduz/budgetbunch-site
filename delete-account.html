Delete your BudgetBunch account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, free of charge.

In the app

  1. Open BudgetBunch and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Read the list of what will happen to your data, then confirm.

Without the app

Write to privacy@budgetbunch.com from the email address of your account and ask for it to be deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your account, your profile and your profile photo
  • Your personal expenses
  • Your household with its budget, if you were its only member
  • Events you created, if nobody else in them has an account
  • The notes you wrote on expenses in a shared household
  • Analytics events linked to your account, within about a week

What stays

  • Expenses you recorded in a shared household keep their amount, currency, category, sub-category and date, because the other members’ totals depend on them. Your name is replaced with “Deleted account”.
  • A shared household and its budget pass to another member. An event you created passes to a participant who has an account.
  • Your name in other people’s events is replaced in the same way.

Deleted records can remain in database backups until those are overwritten. The Privacy Policy has the full detail.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.
