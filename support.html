Support

Write to support@budgetbunch.com. One person answers every message. You can also send feedback from inside the app, under Profile, then Give Feedback.

Households

How do I join someone’s household?

Ask them for their invitation code. In the app, open Profile, then Invite & Join, enter the code and press Connect. Joining is free.

My code is valid but I can’t join

A household holds four people at most, including its owner, and you can be in one shared household at a time. If you are already in one, leave it first.

How do I leave a household?

Leave it from the Invite & Join screen. You stop seeing its expenses and it stops seeing yours. Expenses you already added stay with the household, because the other members’ totals depend on them. The owner cannot leave. Deleting the owner’s account hands the household to the other members.

Budgets and currency

Why can’t I change the currency?

The currency is fixed once a household sets its first limit, so that every amount is compared in the same currency.

Why is the amount left different from my own spending?

The budget counts the whole household. The list underneath can be narrowed to one person, and that changes only the list.

Your data

How do I export my data?

Open Profile, then Export my data. You get a spreadsheet of your expenses and a JSON file with everything, including limits and planner events. Exporting is free.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on Delete your account.

How do I turn analytics off?

Use the usage data switch in Profile. Turning it off also deletes what was already sent.

Privacy requests
