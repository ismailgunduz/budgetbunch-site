      Questions

      Does everyone in the household have to pay?

      No. Joining is always free, and people in your household can use its premium features as long as one member pays. The planner is the exception: each person needs their own membership to add to it.

      How do I invite someone?

      Open Profile, then Invite & Join, and share your code. They enter it on the same screen on their phone.

      Can the others see everything I add?

      Yes. A household has one expense list that all its members can read, so join only with people you trust with it. Only you can edit or remove what you recorded.

      Which phones does it run on?

      iPhone and Android, with the same account on both. The app is in English.

      Who can use it?

      Anyone aged 16 or older.
